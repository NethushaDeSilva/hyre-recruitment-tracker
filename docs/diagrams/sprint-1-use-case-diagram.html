Business rule (not diagrammed line-by-line to avoid clutter): within Recruitment Board & Review, who may actually Move Candidate to Next Stage, Reject Candidate or Hire Candidate for a given candidate depends on which role owns that candidate's current stage on that position (HR owns Applied/Screening, Interviewer owns Department/Interview stages, Management owns the Final stage) — Management may act on any stage regardless of ownership. Post Review & Score and View Recruitment Board are open to all three Staff roles regardless of stage ownership. Reconsider Rejected Candidate and Filter Candidates with AI are HR-only; Compare Candidates is HR & Interviewer only (not Management); Search & Filter Candidates, Download CV and View Employees Roster are HR & Management only (not Interviewer).
